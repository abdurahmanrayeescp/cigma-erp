# CIGMA ERP: Student & Parent Guide

Welcome to the CIGMA Portal. This guide helps students and parents navigate the digital ecosystem.

## 1. App Installation
- You do not need the App Store or Google Play. Simply open `portal.creativecigma.com` on your mobile browser (Safari or Chrome), and click the "Install App" prompt that appears. This will add the CIGMA Portal to your home screen for offline-capable access.

## 2. Tracking Academics
- **AI Analytics**: Check the Results and Attendance modules to see smart, human-readable insights about performance trends generated dynamically by the CIGMA AI engine.
- **Library**: Browse the school's Book Catalog and check due dates for issued material.
- **Homework**: Track daily assignments directly from your mobile device.

## 3. Operations
- **Transport**: Parents can view their child's assigned bus route, driver details, and contact numbers via the Transport module.
- **Fees**: Track current fee statuses to ensure no pending balances.
