# CIGMA ERP: Teacher Guide

Welcome to the CIGMA Portal. This guide explains how to manage your daily academic responsibilities efficiently.

## 1. Daily Operations
- **Attendance**: Navigate to the Attendance module to quickly mark daily registers. Absences are automatically recorded and sent to the AI Analytics engine to monitor chronic absenteeism.
- **Marks Entry**: Input test and examination marks for your assigned classes. Once submitted, the Marks AI engine will evaluate class performance trends.

## 2. Academics & Assignments
- **Homework**: Assign homework securely. Students will see deadlines directly in their portal.
- **Timetable**: Check your daily routing and class assignments via the Timetable module.

## 3. Human Resources
- **Payslips**: Access the Payroll module to securely view your monthly net pay, track allowances and deductions, and download official PDF payslips for your records.
